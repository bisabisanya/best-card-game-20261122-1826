Ember Duel

A duel of the four elements. Outwit the Gloom Warden,
spend your mana well, and bring their life to zero.

EMBER TIDE STONE GALE

How to play

  • Both duelists start with 30 life. Bring the Gloom Warden to 0 to win.
  • Each turn you gain +1 mana (up to 10) and draw a card.
  • Click a card (or press 1–7) to play it. It costs the mana shown on it.
  • Ember burns, Tide heals and draws, Stone shields and crushes, Gale is fast and sharp.
  • Shield absorbs damage before life does. Press Space to end your turn.
